# Sale cost estimates and profit scope

Migration 099 adds immutable `SalesInvoiceCostParts` and `SalesReturnEstimatedCosts`. New sale posting writes a part for every actual FIFO allocation and a separate part for each uncovered stock-document quantity in the same transaction as the invoice. The latter uses the most recent valid receipt cost known at posting, preferring the invoice warehouse, then another warehouse, then the configured variant/product purchase price. A configured zero is a valid explicit cost; absent cost is saved as `unknown` with a null value. Purchase receipts posted after the sale are never used. Reversal documents and sales returns are excluded from purchase-cost selection. No stock movement or batch is created for an uncovered sale.

An actual part references its original FIFO allocation and movement; every part references the sale line and original inventory document line. The records cannot be updated or deleted and the allocation reference is unique. Existing posted invoices are not backfilled. Historical reporting keeps the prior FIFO evidence path and leaves incomplete records unavailable.

Linked returns restore only units actually issued from stock, retaining the original layer and expiry order. Uncovered units do not create an inventory receipt on return. `SalesReturnEstimatedCosts` records the cumulative proportional reversal of their original saved estimate, including repeated partial returns and the final rounding residual. Cancellations and corrections reverse only original physical movements, while financial report events reverse the saved sale cost. Idempotent operation keys and the existing transaction boundaries remain in force.

Gross profit is tax-exclusive saved sales revenue, including saved line and invoice discounts, less saved actual and estimated COGS, net of linked returns and cancellations. Product profitability uses the same sale/return evidence and does not allocate operating expenses. The net-profit report and Reports Center show **operating profit for recorded sales**: gross profit less vouchers currently classified as included operating expenses. Expense recognition follows voucher posting UTC, with the original business expense date displayed. Treasury transfers, supplier payments, customer settlements, loan principal and inventory purchases are not operating expenses in this query. The result does not claim complete business-wide net profit from maintenance, installment interest, or other unmodeled income. Missing cost or unclassified vouchers makes the reported net amount unavailable.

`estimatedCogs` / `estimatedCost` expose the portion of included COGS based on a purchase-cost estimate. The gross-profit figure remains one total; the indicator prevents readers from mistaking all its costs for physical-stock allocations. Cashier warnings are inline and never change stock permissions.
