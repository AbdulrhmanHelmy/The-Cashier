# Installment sales

New installment sales use `POST /api/sales/installments`. One SQLite transaction creates the sales invoice and lines, calls the ordinary sales inventory/FIFO poster, writes one customer receivable, creates the linked contract and schedule, and records any cash down payment in both the invoice/customer ledger and the selected cash treasury account. It also posts the full contract amount including interest as a debit to the separate installment receivable ledger and the down payment as a credit. Every later collection credits that ledger while its actual cash/bank movement is posted to the selected treasury. A debt-reducing return credits the receivable ledger; cash refunds remain in the sales-return treasury. The ledger is append-only and does not contribute to cash/bank balances. A failure at any stage rolls back the entire transaction. The operation key is unique and an identical retry returns the original invoice and contract. The same key with different sale, unit, tracking, payment, or schedule data conflicts.

Migration 090 adds nullable, unique `CustomerInstallmentContracts.SalesInvoiceId`; historical contracts remain unlinked and unchanged. Invoice reads expose `installmentContractId`, contract reads expose `salesInvoiceId`, and posted line reads expose the inventory document's saved unit conversion. Migration 091 adds append-only partial payments. Historical full payments remain in their original table. Schedule reads, reports, and alerts aggregate both tables. Collections update the customer ledger, invoice paid amount, treasury account, and shift in one transaction without touching stock. An optional payment `amount` permits partial collection; omission collects the remaining row amount. Overpayment and duplicate retries are rejected or replayed respectively.

The installment screen searches the server catalog by name, SKU, or barcode; it loads one selected product's active variants and selling units, displays warehouse stock, and sends the selected unit, quantity, price, and optional tracking facts to the atomic endpoint. The existing sales validation and inventory plan apply the current per-product out-of-stock policy, base-unit conversion, batch/expiry/serial identity rules, and real FIFO layers. The browser preview is guidance; posting revalidates inside the transaction.

The former `POST /api/customers/{id}/installments` returns `410 legacy_installment_creation_closed` after authentication, CSRF, and screen checks. Historical contracts remain readable and collectible. New integrated contracts accept a flat percentage applied once to the amount financed after the down payment. Interest is stored as hundredths of a percent on the immutable contract; `TotalAmountScaled` and the immutable schedule include invoice value plus interest, while the sales invoice remains at its original total. The customer ledger records the fee as a separate increase in the same posting transaction. Collections pay invoice principal first, then interest; payment rows retain the interest allocation. At contract creation, the operator selects whether returned goods cancel the corresponding proportional interest share or leave it due. When interest is canceled, unpaid interest reduces the schedule and customer debt; already paid interest is either refunded in cash or posted as customer credit according to the return selection. Each adjustment is append-only and atomic with the return. The current screen creates one invoice line at a time. Linked invoice returns use append-only schedule reductions in the same transaction as stock and customer settlement; see [returns-management.md](returns-management.md). Cancellation and posted corrections of linked installment invoices remain blocked. The screen does not yet save an editable installment draft before atomic posting.
